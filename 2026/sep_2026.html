september 2nd

my girlfriend thought a photo i sent to her of the beer i was drinking was a photo of a beer id like to drink. this was some time earlier and not extremely recent. the idea that someone would consider that i am living with a barrier dense enough between desire and action that i am unable to commit to doing something as simple as sitting outside and drinking a beer is and was such a heavy weight on me that it took me far longer to recover from the insult and introspection caused by the assumption that i am unable to do something so simple than it took for me to drink the beer. that beer i was drinking is a beer id like to drink. which is to say, looking at the photo of the beer i was drinking and knowing it was a good decision to order and then drink it, that i am far weaker and give off far more weakness and lack of care towards myself than i would have normally expected.
